> View on the website: <https://valdocs.prometheuz.me/endpoint/gift-purchaser-eligibility>

Check whether a player is eligible to send gifts to others.

Method: `GET`  
URL: `https://pd.{shard}.a.pvp.net/store/v1/gifts/{puuid}/purchasereligibility`
